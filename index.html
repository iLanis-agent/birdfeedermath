Birdfeeder math

The seed bag runs out on the coldest week of the year, the feeder swings empty by noon, and the pole has a squirrel highway on it. Birdfeeder math counts the seed your feeders actually eat, the ports your birds actually queue for, and checks the pole and the window against the numbers that matter. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Seed plan - feeders, fills and weeks become kilograms, bags and spare seed.
  • Port plan - visiting birds become ports and tube feeders.
  • Placement check - pole height and window distance become baffle and strike verdicts.

Labeled norms

Seed: a busy tube feeder takes about 0.75 kg per fill and about 2 refills a week in the season. Ports: about one feeding port per 2 visiting birds; a standard tube has about 6. Placement: a pole baffle sits about 120-150 cm off the ground; hang the feeder within 1 m of glass or past 9 m - the 1-9 m band is the labeled window-strike risk. Labeled backyard-birding references, not rules.
